Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

StepView operates as a decentralized mobile utility tool. Your privacy is paramount to us, which is why our application is designed to function entirely locally without intermediate servers.

1. Data Collection and Transmission

StepView does not collect, harvest, or store any personal information, CAD files (STP/STEP), 3D point cloud scans, or API keys. All processing, calculations, and rendering occur natively on your mobile device.

2. Third-Party AI Integrations (BYOK Architecture)

This app utilizes a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model for its AI features. When you access the AI Assistant for preliminary FEA analysis or 3D object reconstruction, the app sends your temporary engineering data and prompts encrypted via HTTPS directly to your chosen AI vendor (Google AI Studio, Anthropic Claude, or DeepSeek Platform). This data transmission is governed solely by the privacy policies of those respective providers. We do not intermediate, monitor, or retain these packets.

3. Security of Your API Credentials

The private API keys you input are encrypted using Android's hardware-backed cryptographic security (Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences) and remain securely stored on your local device storage. They are never exposed to us or any unauthorized third party.

4. App Permissions

StepView requires camera access exclusively for the 3D scanning feature (to capture real-world object point clouds) and storage access to open and save your native CAD/STP files. These permissions are only used locally.

5. Contact Information

Since we do not collect any user data, we have no user databases to manage. If you have any technical questions regarding this policy, you can contact us via our developer channel on GitHub.
